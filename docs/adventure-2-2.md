## State and ordering

Board owns ordered Starcatchers, coins, unique identities, consumption and credit; actors own serializable motion/hunger/timers. Player claim immediately excludes a star from eaters and collectors. One Starcatcher meal removes its star before spawning a distinct diamond; Clyde credits before immediate removal. Last-Penta removal changes collector eligibility and following Board survival checks. Preserve corpse state and special-diamond phase through reload.

W1 sorts Penta before food/guppies, OtherTypePet later and Coin last (`Board.cpp:1937–1962`). Primary order remains unverified. Constructor/update draw order is part of the source contract, while full retail RNG parity is not established. Keep Clyde separate from Stinky's retraction logic; reuse numeric geometry only where evidence supports it.

Immediate actor transactions matter within one update:final-Penta starvation releases star eligibility to Clyde later that update; a living Penta's newborn special diamond can be collected by Clyde before its first rising Coin update. A nonempty list of ineligible stars still suppresses Clyde wandering. Bought timer controls bubble admission/entrance ymin handling and decrements after its branch, including old1→0; it is distinct from steering delay and alien eligibility.
